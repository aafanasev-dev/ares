## Prompt

Create a photorealistic 16:9 landscape photograph in the style of a nature documentary: drone shot from above the lip
of a gorge, wide-angle lens, natural light, sharp detail, believable scale.

Setting: the planet Ares, real Mars terrain turned into an Earth-like world with oceans, NOT the red Mars of space
photos. The sky is blue with white clouds, the air is breathable, the sun looks the same size as from Earth and there
is no moon. Gravity is only 0.38 g, so life grows about 2.6× taller and lighter than on Earth: trees reach hundreds of
metres on huge buttresses, big animals walk slowly on long, very thin legs, and flyers have giant wingspans. The
planet is small, so the horizon is close. Rain falls in few but very heavy storms. All plants and animals are alien
but believable: no Earth species, no dinosaurs.

Scene: mid-morning on a beaded river, looking along a short, steep gorge that links two lakes at different levels. In
the background lies a wide, perfectly still lake filling an old impact crater, its surface brimming at the lowest
notch in the crater rim. At that notch the water spills over as a waterfall at the head of a narrow rock gorge, which
drops in a stair of white rapids and smaller falls and opens into a second, lower crater lake in the foreground. The
gorge is a shaded slot filled with dense gallery forest, taller and darker than the low scrub and open rainforest on
the flat crater rims on either side, with spray drifting through it. Both crater rims are ringed with forest; the
country beyond is a plain covered in more overlapping crater rings. Flyers with long wings cross between the two
lakes. Humid air, bright sun, a few towering afternoon clouds beginning to build. Composition: the eye travels from
the still upper lake, over the fall, down the green slot of the gorge, to the lower lake.

No people, buildings, roads, vehicles, spacecraft, text, labels, watermarks or borders. No red or orange sky. No Earth
animals.

## Variations

- Show the dry season: the spillway reduced to a trickle over the notch and the two lakes cut off from each other.
- Show it from inside the gorge at water level: wet black rock, white water and the dark gallery canopy overhead.
