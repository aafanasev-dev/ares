## Prompt

Create a photorealistic 16:9 seascape photograph in the style of a nature documentary: high drone shot with a
wide-angle lens, natural light, sharp detail, believable scale.

Setting: the planet Ares, real Mars terrain turned into an Earth-like world with oceans, NOT the red Mars of space
photos. The sky is blue with white clouds, the air is breathable, the sun looks the same size as from Earth and there
is no moon. Gravity is only 0.38 g: ocean waves grow 1.6–2.6× taller and 2.6× longer than on Earth for the same wind
and roll in slowly with very long periods, spray hangs in the air, tides are tiny, and seabird-like flyers have giant
wingspans. The planet is small, so the horizon is close. All life is alien but believable: no Earth species.

Scene: afternoon in the short southern summer over a sea that flooded an ancient, heavily cratered highland. The water
is 200–2,000 m deep, and the rims of drowned craters rise as ring-shaped islands and circular turquoise banks, dozens
of them scattered to the horizon. Some ring islands are forested; others are bare rock with surf breaking on them.
Trade winds raise 2–4 m seas, and swell wraps around the rings. A thunderstorm builds over one of the larger islands.
Around the shallow rims, reef-like communities of attached organisms colour the water. Composition: a ring island with
a turquoise inner lagoon in the foreground, more crater rings and banks in the middle, the storm and islands to the
close horizon.

No people, boats, ships, buildings, vehicles, spacecraft, text, labels, watermarks or borders. No red or orange sky. No
Earth animals.
