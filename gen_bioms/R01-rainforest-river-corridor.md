# R01 · Rainforest river corridor and gravel braids

Description: `biomes.md` → R01. Paste the text under **Prompt** into ChatGPT to generate the image.

## Prompt

Create a photorealistic 16:9 landscape photograph in the style of a nature documentary: low drone shot with a
wide-angle lens, natural light, sharp detail, believable scale.

Setting: the planet Ares, real Mars terrain turned into an Earth-like world with oceans, NOT the red Mars of space
photos. The sky is blue with white clouds, the air is breathable, the sun looks the same size as from Earth and there
is no moon. Gravity is only 0.38 g, so life grows about 2.6× taller and lighter than on Earth: trees reach hundreds of
metres on huge buttresses, big animals walk slowly on long, very thin legs, and flyers have giant wingspans. The
planet is small, so the horizon is close. Rain falls in few but very heavy storms. All plants and animals are alien
but believable: no Earth species, no dinosaurs.

Scene: late morning, flying low along a huge braided river through lowland rainforest, looking downstream. The river
is milky brown and opaque with silt, far wider and shallower than an Earth river of the same flow, split by pale
gravel bars into many shifting channels. On both banks a solid wall of rainforest 150–250 m tall rises straight out of
the water on enormous buttressed trunks, so the river reads as a bright corridor cut through a dark green canyon of
leaves. On the fresh gravel bars stand thickets of fast-growing pioneer trees 20–40 m tall, all the same age and
height, with whole bars scoured bare where the last flood passed. Very tall, extremely thin-legged wading animals fish
the shallows at mid-channel, and flyers with giant wingspans cross the gap between the canopy walls. Thin morning
river fog is still lifting in strips over the water; humid, hazy air; towering clouds building above the forest.
Composition: the braided channel leading the eye from the foreground into the distance between the two forest walls.

No people, buildings, roads, vehicles, spacecraft, text, labels, watermarks or borders. No red or orange sky. No Earth
animals.

## Variations

- Show the flood peak: the bars drowned, the water brown and fast, whole trees riding the current.
- Show dawn from the water: dense fog on the river, the canopy walls as dark silhouettes above it.
