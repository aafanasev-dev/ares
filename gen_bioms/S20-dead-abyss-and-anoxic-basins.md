## Prompt

Create a photorealistic 16:9 underwater photograph in the style of a deep-sea nature documentary: wide-angle lens,
dim deep-water light with faint bioluminescence, sharp detail, believable scale.

Setting: underwater in the ocean of the planet Ares, an Earth-like ocean world built on real Mars terrain. Gravity is
only 0.38 g: water pressure rises 2.6× more slowly with depth than on Earth, so animals with gas-filled swim bladders
and air-breathing divers live kilometres down, and particles of marine snow sink 2.6× more slowly, filling the water
like slow snowfall. All life is alien but believable: no Earth species.

Scene: near the bottom of a deep enclosed basin, several kilometres down, in warm but lifeless water. A sharp,
shimmering boundary layer crosses the frame: above it, faintly lit water with a few drifting particles and a lone
bioluminescent animal; below it, murky, sulfidic, oxygen-free water tinged brownish-green where nothing lives. The
floor is a flat, starved plain of fine grey sediment with ripple marks, a few ancient sunken tree trunks and no animal
tracks. The only light comes from the faint glow of the animal above the boundary. The mood is silent, still and
empty. Composition: bare sediment plain in the foreground, the shimmering boundary layer across the middle, the glowing
animal above it.

No people, divers, submarines, equipment, text, labels, watermarks or borders. No Earth animals.
