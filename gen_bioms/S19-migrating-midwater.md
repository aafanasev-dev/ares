## Prompt

Create a photorealistic 16:9 underwater photograph in the style of a deep-sea nature documentary: wide-angle lens,
natural deep-water light with bioluminescence, sharp detail, believable scale.

Setting: underwater in the ocean of the planet Ares, an Earth-like ocean world built on real Mars terrain. Gravity is
only 0.38 g: water pressure rises 2.6× more slowly with depth than on Earth, so animals with gas-filled swim bladders
and air-breathing divers live kilometres down, and particles of marine snow sink 2.6× more slowly, filling the water
like slow snowfall. All life is alien but believable: no Earth species.

Scene: dusk, about 1,000 m deep in the open ocean, looking up toward the faint deep-blue glow of the surface. A vast
column of animals is rising from the depths on its daily migration of 2–3 km toward the surface: shoals of swim-
bladdered fish, drifting jelly-like forms and larger hunters, spiralling upward in layers. Dense, slow marine snow
hangs everywhere in the water. Big, delicate suspension feeders spread fans and nets to catch the drifting particles.
A large air-breathing diving animal descends past the rising crowd. Scattered blue-green bioluminescent sparks flicker
through the scene. Composition: a suspension feeder's fan in the foreground, the rising column of animals in the
middle, the faint surface glow above.

No people, divers, submarines, equipment, text, labels, watermarks or borders. No Earth animals.
