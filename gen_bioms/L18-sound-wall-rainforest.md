## Prompt

Create a photorealistic 16:9 landscape photograph in the style of a nature documentary: wide-angle lens from high on a
cliff, natural light, sharp detail, believable scale.

Setting: the planet Ares, real Mars terrain turned into an Earth-like world with oceans, NOT the red Mars of space
photos. The sky is blue with white clouds, the air is breathable, the sun looks the same size as from Earth and there
is no moon. Gravity is only 0.38 g, so life grows about 2.6× taller and lighter than on Earth: trees reach hundreds of
metres on huge buttresses, big animals walk slowly on long, very thin legs, and flyers have giant wingspans. The
planet is small, so the horizon is close. Rain falls in few but very heavy storms. All plants and animals are alien
but believable: no Earth species, no dinosaurs.

Scene: afternoon, looking along a flooded canyon sound 150 km wide and 4,000 km long, from high on its southern wall.
The walls rise 3–5 km straight out of the water, cut by side gorges and exposed rocky spurs; the far wall is visible
across the water, and the sound stretches away to the close horizon. Wind funnels along the axis and streaks the water
with whitecaps. The walls are clothed in rainforest: towering giants packed into the sheltered side gorges, and low,
wind-pruned scrub on the exposed spurs. A line of cliff-nesting giant soaring flyers with 20 m wingspans rides the
ridge lift along the wall without flapping. Towering clouds hug the walls, heavy rain curtains hang toward the west
(the canyon's head), and sunlight falls on the water in moving patches. Composition: a wind-pruned spur in the
foreground, soarers along the wall in the middle, the long sound and rain curtains beyond.

No people, buildings, roads, vehicles, spacecraft, text, labels, watermarks or borders. No red or orange sky. No Earth
animals.

## Variations

- Show dawn: calm water, cloud just forming on the walls, soarers resting on ledges.
- Show the rain wall at full strength: grey curtains swallowing the canyon.
