## Prompt

Create a photorealistic 16:9 landscape photograph in the style of a nature documentary: high drone shot with a
wide-angle lens, natural light, sharp detail, believable scale.

Setting: the planet Ares, real Mars terrain turned into an Earth-like world with oceans, NOT the red Mars of space
photos. The sky is blue with white clouds, the air is breathable, the sun looks the same size as from Earth and there
is no moon. Gravity is only 0.38 g, so life grows about 2.6× taller and lighter than on Earth: trees reach hundreds of
metres on huge buttresses, big animals walk slowly on long, very thin legs, and flyers have giant wingspans. The
planet is small, so the horizon is close. Rain falls in few but very heavy storms. All plants and animals are alien
but believable: no Earth species, no dinosaurs.

Scene: late afternoon in the dry season, high above a river crossing an enormous brown plain. A single dense, deep
green ribbon of evergreen gallery forest 40–80 m tall follows every bend of the river and stops dead a short distance
from the water, where the ground turns to dry, leafless savanna: pale 5–8 m grasses and scattered flat-crowned,
heavily buttressed trees standing bare. The contrast is absolute, a green line drawn across a brown plain. The river
itself is braided and turbid, narrowing downstream as the dry land drinks it. Herds of very large, slow animals on
extremely long thin legs are strung along the bank coming down to drink, with reed beds and backwaters beside them; a
tall dust devil several kilometres high crosses the open plain in the distance. Warm, dusty, golden low light; a few
high clouds. Composition: the winding green ribbon leading from the foreground to the horizon across the brown plain.

No people, buildings, roads, vehicles, spacecraft, text, labels, watermarks or borders. No red or orange sky. No Earth
animals.

## Variations

- Show the wet season: the plain green, the river in flood and twice as wide, storm clouds piling up.
- Show dawn at ground level on the bank: valley fog under the gallery trees and animals crowding the water.
