## Prompt

Create a photorealistic 16:9 landscape photograph in the style of a nature documentary: low aerial shot just off a
lake shore, wide-angle lens, natural light, sharp detail, believable scale.

Setting: the planet Ares, real Mars terrain turned into an Earth-like world with oceans, NOT the red Mars of space
photos. The sky is blue with white clouds, the air is breathable, the sun looks the same size as from Earth and there
is no moon. Gravity is only 0.38 g, so life grows about 2.6× taller and lighter than on Earth: trees reach hundreds of
metres on huge buttresses, big animals walk slowly on long, very thin legs, and flyers have giant wingspans. The
planet is small, so the horizon is close. Rain falls in few but very heavy storms. All plants and animals are alien
but believable: no Earth species, no dinosaurs.

Scene: a windy afternoon on the shore of a freshwater inland sea so large that the far side is over the horizon, held
in a high basin with no outlet to the ocean. Long, slow waves two to five metres high roll in from open water and
break on a wave-cut cliff and a long curving gravel beach; the water is clear and cool, not salty, with no seaweed,
no tide line and no shells. The near shore is dry, pale bunch-grass steppe and low cushion plants running right to the
beach crest, while across a bay in the distance the opposite shore is a dark wall of rainforest climbing a vast
escarpment into cloud: one lake with a desert coast and a rainforest coast. Whitecaps stretch to the horizon; spray
blows off the wave crests in the low gravity. Flyers with very long wings work the line of the surf. Bright, dry,
sharp air; the sun low enough to put a silver band on the water. Composition: the beach curving from the foreground
into the distance, open water filling the upper half of the frame.

No people, buildings, roads, vehicles, spacecraft, text, labels, watermarks or borders. No red or orange sky. No Earth
animals.

## Variations

- Show dawn calm: the lake like glass, mist over the water and the far escarpment catching the first light.
- Show it from very high up: the whole inland sea in its basin, steppe on one side, forested escarpment on the other.
