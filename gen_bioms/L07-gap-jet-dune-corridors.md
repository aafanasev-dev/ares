## Prompt

Create a photorealistic 16:9 landscape photograph in the style of a nature documentary: low-angle wide shot, natural
light, sharp detail, believable scale.

Setting: the planet Ares, real Mars terrain turned into an Earth-like world with oceans, NOT the red Mars of space
photos. The sky is blue with white clouds, the air is breathable, the sun looks the same size as from Earth and there
is no moon. Gravity is only 0.38 g, so life grows about 2.6× taller and lighter than on Earth: trees reach hundreds of
metres on huge buttresses, big animals walk slowly on long, very thin legs, and flyers have giant wingspans. The
planet is small, so the horizon is close. Rain falls in few but very heavy storms. All plants and animals are alien
but believable: no Earth species, no dinosaurs.

Scene: afternoon in a wind corridor 4–5 km high between two colossal shield volcanoes, whose snow-capped flanks rise on
both sides of the frame. Down the corridor march giant star dunes up to 500 m high, spaced about 15 km apart,
repeating toward the horizon. Between them lies dark, wind-scoured rock pavement. There are no plants at all. A
permanent fierce wind tears long plumes of sand off every dune crest, grit streams across the ground and the sky is
streaked with blown dust, yet the light is hard and bright. In the foreground a low, flattened, burrowing animal
presses itself against the rock; high above, one huge flyer banks as it rides the jet. Composition: scoured rock in the
foreground, the line of star dunes receding through the corridor, volcano flanks framing both sides.

No people, buildings, roads, vehicles, spacecraft, text, labels, watermarks or borders. No red or orange sky. No Earth
animals.

## Variations

- Show the jet at its afternoon peak: a sand haze hides the volcanoes completely.
- Show a calm early morning: sharp dune crests, long shadows, clear air.
