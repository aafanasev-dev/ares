Create a photorealistic 16:9 landscape photograph in the style of a nature documentary: wide-angle lens close to the
ground, natural light, sharp detail, believable scale.

Setting: the planet Ares, real Mars terrain turned into an Earth-like world with oceans, NOT the red Mars of space
photos. The sky is blue with white clouds, the air is breathable, the sun looks the same size as from Earth and there

Scene: noon on a vast, perfectly flat salt flat just a metre or two above sea level — a wasteland that is not dry but

brine pools coloured by pink, orange and green microbial mats. Nothing grows. The sea is a thin bright line on the
horizon, and a low, eroded volcanic cone rises far away. A few long-legged flyers pick food at the edge of a brine pool.
The air is warm (about 25 °C), humid and hazy, with a blinding white glare and heat shimmer. Composition: a brine pool
with microbial colours in the foreground, the endless damp crust in the middle, hazy sea line and distant cone behind.

No people, buildings, roads, vehicles, spacecraft, text, labels, watermarks or borders. No red or orange sky. No Earth
animals.
