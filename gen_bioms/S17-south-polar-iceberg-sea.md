# S17 · South polar iceberg sea

Description: `biomes.md` → S17. Paste the text under **Prompt** into ChatGPT to generate the image.

## Prompt

Create a photorealistic 16:9 seascape photograph in the style of a nature documentary: wide-angle lens low over the
water, natural light, sharp detail, believable scale.

Setting: the planet Ares, real Mars terrain turned into an Earth-like world with oceans, NOT the red Mars of space
photos. The sky is blue with white clouds, the air is breathable, the sun looks the same size as from Earth and there
is no moon. Gravity is only 0.38 g: ocean waves grow 1.6–2.6× taller and 2.6× longer than on Earth for the same wind
and roll in slowly with very long periods, spray hangs in the air, tides are tiny, and seabird-like flyers have giant
wingspans. The planet is small, so the horizon is close. All life is alien but believable: no Earth species.

Scene: the short, fierce southern summer in the sea at the foot of a south polar ice cap that is melting and
retreating. Icebergs of every size float in dark water: small bergy bits near the camera, larger jagged blue-white
bergs further out, and in the distance the tall ice cliffs of an outlet glacier, one slab crashing into the sea. A
layer of fresh meltwater makes the calm surface slightly milky and green with a plankton bloom. A cold wind blows off
the ice cap, rippling the water. The low polar sun stays near the horizon, lighting the bergs gold on one side and
blue in shadow. Giant flyers feed at the bloom. Composition: a small berg in the foreground, larger bergs in the
middle, the calving glacier front behind.

No people, boats, ships, buildings, vehicles, spacecraft, text, labels, watermarks or borders. No red or orange sky. No
Earth animals.

## Variations

- Show the long winter: new sea ice locking the icebergs in place under polar twilight.
- Show an underwater view beneath an iceberg, lit blue from above.
