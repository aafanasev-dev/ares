# L01 · Lunae coastal rainforest

## Prompt

Create a photorealistic 16:9 landscape photograph in the style of a nature documentary: drone shot with a wide-angle
lens, natural light, sharp detail, believable scale.

Setting: the planet Ares, real Mars terrain turned into an Earth-like world with oceans, NOT the red Mars of space
photos. The sky is blue with white clouds, the air is breathable, the sun looks the same size as from Earth and there
is no moon. Gravity is only 0.38 g, so life grows about 2.6× taller and lighter than on Earth: trees reach hundreds of
metres on huge buttresses, big animals walk slowly on long, very thin legs, and flyers have giant wingspans. The
planet is small, so the horizon is close. Rain falls in few but very heavy storms. All plants and animals are alien
but believable: no Earth species, no dinosaurs.

Scene: mid-afternoon, seen from just offshore, looking west at a low tropical coastal plain at the foot of a colossal
dark escarpment that rises 5 km into banked cloud. Heavy surf breaks on a straight shore of dark volcanic sand and
black rock. A brown, braided river ends in a wave-built, cuspate mouth — no estuary, no mudflats. Dense evergreen
lowland rainforest covers the plain: along the exposed seafront the canopy is wind-pruned into a low sculpted wall,
while in sheltered valleys behind it emergent giants 150–200 m tall stand on plank buttresses. Pale gliding animals
with membrane wings drift between the giant crowns, tiny against them. Hot, humid air; towering cumulus piles against
the escarpment and a grey curtain of rain is starting to fall over the forest, while the sea is still in bright sun.
Composition: surf line in the foreground, forest in the middle, cloud-wrapped escarpment behind.

No people, buildings, roads, vehicles, spacecraft, text, labels, watermarks or borders. No red or orange sky. No Earth
animals.

## Variations

- Show the late-afternoon cloudburst: rain hides the escarpment and the rivers run brown into the surf.
- Show a clear early morning: the forest steams and the first cloud is just forming against the wall.
