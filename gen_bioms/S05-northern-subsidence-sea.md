# S05 · Northern subsidence sea

Description: `biomes.md` → S05. Paste the text under **Prompt** into ChatGPT to generate the image.

## Prompt

Create a photorealistic 16:9 seascape photograph in the style of a nature documentary: wide-angle lens just above the
water, natural light, sharp detail, believable scale.

Setting: the planet Ares, real Mars terrain turned into an Earth-like world with oceans, NOT the red Mars of space
photos. The sky is blue with white clouds, the air is breathable, the sun looks the same size as from Earth and there
is no moon. Gravity is only 0.38 g: ocean waves grow 1.6–2.6× taller and 2.6× longer than on Earth for the same wind
and roll in slowly with very long periods, spray hangs in the air, tides are tiny, and seabird-like flyers have giant
wingspans. The planet is small, so the horizon is close. All life is alien but believable: no Earth species.

Scene: a clear, calm afternoon on the open ocean at about 50° north, under sinking dry air. The sky is completely
cloudless and deep blue. The wind is weak, so the surface is nearly glassy, but long, low swells arrive from a distant
storm belt to the north, rising and falling as smooth, slow hills. The water is extremely clear, cold-looking blue, and
a faint salty haze lies along the close horizon from strong evaporation. Life is sparse: a single giant flyer rests on
the water, reflected in a swell. Composition: mirror-like swell in the foreground, the resting flyer in the middle,
empty sea and hazy horizon under a cloudless sky.

No people, boats, ships, buildings, vehicles, spacecraft, text, labels, watermarks or borders. No red or orange sky. No
Earth animals.

## Variations

- Show a starry, moonless night over the calm sea with stars reflected in the swell.
- Show winter: a pale low sun and cold grey-blue water under the same cloudless sky.
