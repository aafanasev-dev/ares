## Prompt

Create a photorealistic 16:9 landscape photograph in the style of a nature documentary: very high drone shot looking
almost straight down at the sea, wide-angle lens, natural light, sharp detail, believable scale.

Setting: the planet Ares, real Mars terrain turned into an Earth-like world with oceans, NOT the red Mars of space
photos. The sky is blue with white clouds, the air is breathable, the sun looks the same size as from Earth and there
is no moon. Gravity is only 0.38 g, so life grows about 2.6× taller and lighter than on Earth: trees reach hundreds of
metres on huge buttresses, big animals walk slowly on long, very thin legs, and flyers have giant wingspans. The
planet is small, so the horizon is close. Rain falls in few but very heavy storms. All plants and animals are alien
but believable: no Earth species, no dinosaurs.

Scene: midday far out at sea, over the front where a huge river plume meets the open ocean. A vast sheet of opaque
milky-brown fresh water lies on top of the sea and stretches back towards a low coastline at the very edge of the
frame; it ends in a sharp, sinuous, clearly drawn front, with deep blue-green ocean on the other side. The boundary is
knife-edged and curls into long swirls and eddies where the two waters shear past each other. The brown side is
slick and glassy, the short chop damped flat; the blue side is wind-roughened with small whitecaps, so the two halves
of the picture have completely different textures. Along the front itself runs a narrow band of bright green bloom,
and there the life gathers: long-winged flyers diving in numbers and large marine animals breaking the surface, all
strung out in a line following the edge. A second, older plume front from another river crosses in the distance.
Bright sun with glitter on the blue water. Composition: the curving front cutting diagonally across the frame, brown
on one side, blue on the other, the green bloom line between them.

No people, buildings, roads, vehicles, spacecraft, text, labels, watermarks or borders. No red or orange sky. No Earth
animals.

## Variations

- Show the flood season: the plume filling almost the whole frame, the front pushed far out and ragged.
- Show it from sea level: a sharp brown-to-blue line on the water with feeding animals working along it.
