## Prompt

Create a photorealistic 16:9 landscape photograph in the style of a nature documentary: wide-angle lens, natural
light, sharp detail, believable scale.

Setting: the planet Ares, real Mars terrain turned into an Earth-like world with oceans, NOT the red Mars of space
photos. The sky is blue with white clouds, the air is breathable, the sun looks the same size as from Earth and there
is no moon. Gravity is only 0.38 g, so life grows about 2.6× taller and lighter than on Earth: trees reach hundreds of
metres on huge buttresses, big animals walk slowly on long, very thin legs, and flyers have giant wingspans. The
planet is small, so the horizon is close. Rain falls in few but very heavy storms. All plants and animals are alien
but believable: no Earth species, no dinosaurs.

Scene: early morning about 9 km up on the summit slopes of a colossal shield volcano, above the treeline and below
the snowline. Bare lava fields, cinder, scree and frost-shattered black and grey rock stretch in every direction. In
rock cracks grow scattered cushion plants and low, tough scrub, thinning to nothing uphill, where the white edge of an
ice cap begins. The slope falls away to a sea of clouds far below; the planet is small, so the horizon beyond the
clouds is close and gently curved. The sky is a deeper blue than at sea level. Frost glitters on the rocks and is
melting in strong sunlight, a stiff wind blows, and the air is extremely clear. A few small, hardy grazers pick at the
cushions, and a giant soaring flyer passes below the viewpoint, silhouetted against the clouds. Composition: frosty
cushion plants in the foreground, lava slope in the middle, cloud sea and ice edge in the background.

No people, buildings, roads, vehicles, spacecraft, text, labels, watermarks or borders. No red or orange sky. No Earth
animals.
