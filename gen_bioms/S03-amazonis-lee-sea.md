## Prompt

Create a photorealistic 16:9 photograph in the style of a nature documentary: very high aerial view looking down at
an angle, natural light, sharp detail, believable scale.

Setting: the planet Ares, real Mars terrain turned into an Earth-like world with oceans, NOT the red Mars of space
photos. The sky is blue with white clouds, the air is breathable, the sun looks the same size as from Earth and there
is no moon. Gravity is only 0.38 g: ocean waves grow 1.6–2.6× taller and 2.6× longer than on Earth for the same wind
and roll in slowly with very long periods, spray hangs in the air, tides are tiny, and seabird-like flyers have giant
wingspans. The planet is small, so the horizon is close. All life is alien but believable: no Earth species.

Scene: midday over a very clear, deep-blue, salty ocean in the lee of a vast continent. On one side of the frame
rises a colossal island volcano, 19 km high and white-capped, standing above a field of low marine clouds. Downwind of
it the clouds are torn apart into a long cloud-free wake stretching toward the horizon, bordered on both sides by a von
Kármán vortex street — alternating spiral eddies of white cloud peeling off the mountain. The sea inside the wake is
sunny, calm-looking and intensely blue, with long, gentle swell lines visible from above. Composition: the volcano at
one edge, the vortex street and clear wake running diagonally across the frame, the curved horizon beyond.

No people, boats, ships, buildings, vehicles, spacecraft, text, labels, watermarks or borders. No red or orange sky. No
Earth animals.

## Variations

- Show the sea surface itself: clear blue water, light wind, slow swell, a giant flyer overhead.
- Show sunrise over the wake, with the vortex clouds lit pink.
