## Prompt

Create a photorealistic 16:9 seascape photograph in the style of a nature documentary: wide-angle lens from a low
coastal point, natural light, sharp detail, believable scale.

Setting: the planet Ares, real Mars terrain turned into an Earth-like world with oceans, NOT the red Mars of space
photos. The sky is blue with white clouds, the air is breathable, the sun looks the same size as from Earth and there
is no moon. Gravity is only 0.38 g: ocean waves grow 1.6–2.6× taller and 2.6× longer than on Earth for the same wind
and roll in slowly with very long periods, spray hangs in the air, tides are tiny, and seabird-like flyers have giant
wingspans. The planet is small, so the horizon is close. All life is alien but believable: no Earth species.

Scene: morning on a vast, enclosed gulf 2,300 km across — the deepest basin on the planet, over 10 km deep — under dry,
sinking air. The water is calm, with only small ripples, and so clear near shore that it shades quickly from turquoise
to an almost black, bottomless blue a short way out. Low banks of white fog lie on the water along the arid shore and
drift onto a pale belt of salt crust. The sky above is cloudless and the morning is already warm. Life at the surface is
sparse but present: a flyer skims the water. Composition: salt crust and still shallows in the foreground, fog banks
over the dark water in the middle, the calm gulf stretching to a close horizon under a cloudless sky.

No people, boats, ships, buildings, vehicles, spacecraft, text, labels, watermarks or borders. No red or orange sky. No
Earth animals.

## Variations

- Show an underwater cross-section: a thin, living blue surface layer above a sharp shimmering boundary and black,
  sulfidic, lifeless water below.
- Show a hot, calm afternoon: glassy water and heat haze over the arid shore.
