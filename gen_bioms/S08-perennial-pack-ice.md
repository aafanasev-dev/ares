# S08 · Perennial pack ice

Description: `biomes.md` → S08. Paste the text under **Prompt** into ChatGPT to generate the image.

## Prompt

Create a photorealistic 16:9 landscape photograph in the style of a nature documentary: wide-angle lens a few metres
above the ice, natural light, sharp detail, believable scale.

Setting: the planet Ares, real Mars terrain turned into an Earth-like world with oceans, NOT the red Mars of space
photos. The sky is blue with white clouds, the air is breathable, the sun looks the same size as from Earth and there
is no moon. Gravity is only 0.38 g: ocean waves grow 1.6–2.6× taller and 2.6× longer than on Earth for the same wind
and roll in slowly with very long periods, spray hangs in the air, tides are tiny, and seabird-like flyers have giant
wingspans. The planet is small, so the horizon is close. All life is alien but believable: no Earth species.

Scene: the permanent pack ice near the north pole of an ocean planet, late in summer when the sun circles low around
the horizon. A vast plain of old, thick sea ice stretches in every direction, broken by jagged pressure ridges of
tilted blue-white blocks and by long, dark, open cracks (leads) of black water where thin new ice is forming. Soft
snow drifts lie in the lee of the ridges. There is no land and no land ice anywhere — only frozen ocean. The light is
low and golden-pink, with long blue shadows; a light easterly wind lifts fine snow. No visible animals.
Composition: a pressure ridge in the foreground, a dark lead crossing the middle, the flat ice plain to the close
horizon.

No people, boats, ships, buildings, vehicles, spacecraft, text, labels, watermarks or borders. No red or orange sky. No
Earth animals.

## Variations

- Show the long polar night: the pack under a starry sky, faint blue light on the snow.
- Show the underside of the ice: algae glowing green in light filtering through a lead.
