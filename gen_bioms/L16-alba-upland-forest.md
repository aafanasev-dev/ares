light, sharp detail, believable scale.

photos. The sky is blue with white clouds, the air is breathable, the sun looks the same size as from Earth and there

that will become great rivers far below. Near the low rim of a shallow summit caldera in the distance, the forest gives
way to open woodland and heath. Grazing animals on long thin legs stand at the bog edge, and a flyer crosses
overhead. Broken clouds race over the upland and long beams of sunlight fall across the meadows. Composition: bog
pools reflecting the sky in the foreground, forest edge in the middle, caldera rim with heath on the horizon.

No people, buildings, roads, vehicles, spacecraft, text, labels, watermarks or borders. No red or orange sky. No Earth
animals.
