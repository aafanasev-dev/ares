# S10 · Side-chasma still water

Description: `biomes.md` → S10. Paste the text under **Prompt** into ChatGPT to generate the image.

## Prompt

Create a photorealistic 16:9 photograph in the style of a nature documentary: wide-angle lens at water level, soft
natural light, sharp detail, believable scale.

Setting: the planet Ares, real Mars terrain turned into an Earth-like world with oceans, NOT the red Mars of space
photos. The sky is blue with white clouds, the air is breathable, the sun looks the same size as from Earth and there
is no moon. Gravity is only 0.38 g: ocean waves grow 1.6–2.6× taller and 2.6× longer than on Earth for the same wind
and roll in slowly with very long periods, spray hangs in the air, tides are tiny, and seabird-like flyers have giant
wingspans. The planet is small, so the horizon is close. All life is alien but believable: no Earth species.

Scene: midday in a sheltered side canyon off a vast flooded canyon system. The water is dead calm and perfectly
glassy, reflecting everything. Canyon walls 4 km high rise into permanent mist. At the water's edge stand trees 300 m
tall with colossal buttress roots, their reflections doubling them. High overhead, enormous catenary sky-nets hundreds
of metres wide hang between the canyon walls, beaded with water droplets that catch soft light, some holding green
drifting mats. The air is warm and saturated, with mist floating low over the water, and a few young fish ripple the
surface in one spot. Composition: the mirror surface with ripples in the foreground, giant tree reflections in the
middle, sky-nets and walls vanishing into mist above.

No people, boats, ships, buildings, vehicles, spacecraft, text, labels, watermarks or borders. No red or orange sky. No
Earth animals.

## Variations

- Show just below the surface: a sunlit nursery of young fish above dark, oxygen-free deep water.
- Show a soaring flyer caught in a sky-net, the net sagging under its weight.
