## Prompt

Create a photorealistic 16:9 landscape photograph in the style of a nature documentary: wide-angle lens, natural
light, sharp detail, believable scale.

Setting: the planet Ares, real Mars terrain turned into an Earth-like world with oceans, NOT the red Mars of space
photos. The sky is blue with white clouds, the air is breathable, the sun looks the same size as from Earth and there
is no moon. Gravity is only 0.38 g, so life grows about 2.6× taller and lighter than on Earth: trees reach hundreds of

planet is small, so the horizon is close. Rain falls in few but very heavy storms. All plants and animals are alien
but believable: no Earth species, no dinosaurs.

Scene: midday on the windward (east) flank of a gigantic shield volcano, about 5–6 km up, looking uphill toward its
ice cap. The slope is gentle but endless, made of old lava flows with black lava cliffs and fast meltwater streams
coming down from the glacier above. A crescent-shaped belt of wet montane forest, 20–60 m tall, wraps this side of the
mountain; uphill it shrinks into twisted krummholz at the treeline near 7 km, then bare rock, then white ice. To one
side the curve of the mountain reveals the dry lee flank in rain shadow — a sharp change from green to bare brown. A
cloud cap sits on the windward slope with cool mist drifting through the trees and a rainbow in the spray, while the
sky over the lee side is clear. Large flyers nest on the lava cliffs. Composition: mossy forest and a stream in the
foreground, treeline and rock in the middle, ice cap above.

No people, buildings, roads, vehicles, spacecraft, text, labels, watermarks or borders. No red or orange sky. No Earth
animals.
