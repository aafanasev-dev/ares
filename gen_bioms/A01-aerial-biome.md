# A01 · Aerial biome

Description: `biomes.md` → A01. Paste the text under **Prompt** into ChatGPT to generate the image.

## Prompt

Create a photorealistic 16:9 photograph in the style of a nature documentary: wide-angle lens at high altitude, as if
from a glider flying beside the animals, natural light, sharp detail, believable scale.

Setting: the sky of the planet Ares, an Earth-like ocean world built on real Mars terrain, NOT the red Mars of space
photos. The sky is deep blue, the sun looks the same size as from Earth and there is no moon. Gravity is only 0.38 g
and the atmosphere is 2.6× deeper than Earth's: at 10 km the air is still thick, damp and about 0 °C, so a whole
ecosystem lives permanently aloft. The planet is small, so the horizon below curves noticeably from high up. All life
is alien but believable: no Earth species.

Scene: about 10 km above a tropical ocean, among giant convective storm towers that rise another 20 km above the
viewpoint. The air is filled with the aerial biosphere: gossamer photosynthesizers — delicate, translucent green
sheets and filaments metres across but weighing only grams — drift and ripple in the updrafts, together forming a
faint green haze. Small aerial grazers with kite-like membranes ride the thermals and feed on the mats. In the
foreground a giant soarer with a 20 m wingspan glides slowly through a cloud of mats with its mouth open. Far below,
the ocean and scattered cumulus curve toward the horizon. Composition: the giant soarer in the foreground, drifting
green sheets and grazers in the middle, storm towers and the curved ocean horizon behind.

No people, aircraft, balloons, buildings, vehicles, spacecraft, text, labels, watermarks or borders. No red or orange
sky. No Earth animals.

## Variations

- Show the edge of a dry subsidence belt: the green haze thinning to clear blue air over a calm sea.
- Show sunset at altitude: backlit green sheets glowing gold around the silhouette of a giant soarer.
