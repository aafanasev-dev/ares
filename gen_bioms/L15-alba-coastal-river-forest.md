## Prompt

Create a photorealistic 16:9 landscape photograph in the style of a nature documentary: high drone shot with a
wide-angle lens, natural light, sharp detail, believable scale.

Setting: the planet Ares, real Mars terrain turned into an Earth-like world with oceans, NOT the red Mars of space
photos. The sky is blue with white clouds, the air is breathable, the sun looks the same size as from Earth and there
is no moon. Gravity is only 0.38 g, so life grows about 2.6× taller and lighter than on Earth: trees reach hundreds of
metres on huge buttresses, big animals walk slowly on long, very thin legs, and flyers have giant wingspans. The
planet is small, so the horizon is close. Rain falls in few but very heavy storms. All plants and animals are alien
but believable: no Earth species, no dinosaurs.

Scene: mid-morning over the largest forest on the planet, on the very gentle slopes of an enormous low volcano facing
a cool northern ocean. A wide, turbid, braided river with pale gravel bars winds through the forest, joined by many
tributaries in a radiating pattern, and ends in a wave-built, cuspate delta pushing into a grey-green sea lined with
surf. The forest canopy is strikingly uniform, 60–100 m tall, with no emergent giants because nothing is sheltered from
the wind; one rectangular patch of lighter, even-aged young forest marks an old windthrow. Large animals stand on a
gravel bar and fish-eating flyers circle over the river. Mild, fresh maritime air; river fog is lifting in thin
bands; scattered clouds. Composition: the river leading from the foreground through the forest to the delta and sea.

No people, buildings, roads, vehicles, spacecraft, text, labels, watermarks or borders. No red or orange sky. No Earth
animals.

## Variations

- Show a windstorm: the canopy bending in waves and high surf at the delta.
- Show the short, mild winter: light rain, grey sea, low cloud over the forest.
