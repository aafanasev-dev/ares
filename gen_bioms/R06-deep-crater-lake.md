# R06 · Deep crater lake

## Prompt

Create a photorealistic 16:9 landscape photograph in the style of a nature documentary: high drone shot looking down
and across, wide-angle lens, natural light, sharp detail, believable scale.

Setting: the planet Ares, real Mars terrain turned into an Earth-like world with oceans, NOT the red Mars of space
photos. The sky is blue with white clouds, the air is breathable, the sun looks the same size as from Earth and there
is no moon. Gravity is only 0.38 g, so life grows about 2.6× taller and lighter than on Earth: trees reach hundreds of
metres on huge buttresses, big animals walk slowly on long, very thin legs, and flyers have giant wingspans. The
planet is small, so the horizon is close. Rain falls in few but very heavy storms. All plants and animals are alien
but believable: no Earth species, no dinosaurs.

Scene: early morning over a perfectly circular lake filling an old impact crater, hundreds of metres deep and dead
calm. The water is dark and glassy, mirror-still, reflecting the sky and the rim; near the shore it is clear enough to
show a steep drop-off going straight down into blackness. A rainforest canopy 150 m tall comes right to the water's
edge all the way round and stops sharply at the circular crater rim, so from above the lake looks like a dark eye
inside a ring of green. Beyond the rim, a cratered plain carries more green rings to the horizon, each its own
isolated world. A short spillway gorge notches the far rim. Patches of low fog still lie on the water; a flock of
long-winged flyers crosses the surface, their reflections under them. Warm, humid, utterly still air. Composition: the
circle of the lake centred and the ring of forest around it, the plain of other crater rings receding behind.

No people, buildings, roads, vehicles, spacecraft, text, labels, watermarks or borders. No red or orange sky. No Earth
animals.

## Variations

- Show the walled version: a lake at the bottom of an enormous canyon with 4 km cliffs, in permanent shadow and mist.
- Show it at water level from the shore: black still water, the wall of forest behind, a drowned tree in the shallows.
