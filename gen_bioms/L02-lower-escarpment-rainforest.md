## Prompt

Create a photorealistic 16:9 landscape photograph in the style of a nature documentary: wide-angle lens from a rock
ledge, natural light, sharp detail, believable scale.

Setting: the planet Ares, real Mars terrain turned into an Earth-like world with oceans, NOT the red Mars of space
photos. The sky is blue with white clouds, the air is breathable, the sun looks the same size as from Earth and there
is no moon. Gravity is only 0.38 g, so life grows about 2.6× taller and lighter than on Earth: trees reach hundreds of
metres on huge buttresses, big animals walk slowly on long, very thin legs, and flyers have giant wingspans. The
planet is small, so the horizon is close. Rain falls in few but very heavy storms. All plants and animals are alien
but believable: no Earth species, no dinosaurs.

Scene: late morning on a steep mountain wall about 1.5 km above the sea, looking along the slope. Deep, narrow gorges
cut the wall, separated by knife-edge ridges. A waterfall several hundred metres tall breaks up into drifting mist
before it reaches the gorge floor. Rainforest grows in stacked bands up the slope: in the sheltered gorges stand
cathedral giants 150–250 m tall, heavy with epiphytes, while only a few hundred metres away the wind-exposed ridge
crests carry low, wind-pruned scrub, with a sharp boundary between the two. A patch of even-aged young trees marks an
old storm blowdown. In one gorge a stilt-wader — a browsing animal 60–80 m tall on four absurdly thin legs — feeds at
mid-canopy height, and small gliding animals cross between crowns. White cloud streamers climb the wall, sunbeams break
through gaps, and everything drips with water. Composition: ridge scrub in the foreground, a gorge of giants in the
middle, cloud-wrapped wall stacking up behind.

No people, buildings, roads, vehicles, spacecraft, text, labels, watermarks or borders. No red or orange sky. No Earth
animals.

## Variations

- Show the evening monsoon downpour: waterfalls swollen, cloud filling the gorges.
- Show dawn: mist lying in the gorges while the ridge crests are clear and sunlit.
