wingspans, many of them wheeling in the air. A band of cloud forms on the flank while the summit stands clear;
the open ocean glitters below. Composition: nesting cliffs in the foreground, forest and glacier tongue in the middle,
the enormous flank and ice cap rising above.

No people, buildings, roads, vehicles, spacecraft, text, labels, watermarks or borders. No red or orange sky. No Earth
