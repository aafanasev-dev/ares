# S01 · Equatorial warm ocean

Description: `biomes.md` → S01. Paste the text under **Prompt** into ChatGPT to generate the image.

## Prompt

Create a photorealistic 16:9 seascape photograph in the style of a nature documentary: wide-angle lens just above the
water, natural light, sharp detail, believable scale.

Setting: the planet Ares, real Mars terrain turned into an Earth-like world with oceans, NOT the red Mars of space
photos. The sky is blue with white clouds, the air is breathable, the sun looks the same size as from Earth and there
is no moon. Gravity is only 0.38 g: ocean waves grow 1.6–2.6× taller and 2.6× longer than on Earth for the same wind
and roll in slowly with very long periods, spray hangs in the air, tides are tiny, and seabird-like flyers have giant
wingspans. The planet is small, so the horizon is close. All life is alien but believable: no Earth species.

Scene: late afternoon in the calm equatorial doldrums of a warm tropical ocean. The local wind is light, so the surface
is glassy, but long, low swells 2–4 m high arrive from far away with very long periods, moving like slow hills of
water. Towering storm clouds 30 km tall with flat anvils stand in a line on the horizon; under one, a squall is
whipping the sea white and pouring a dark curtain of rain. The water is deep blue, the air hot and humid, and a faint
green haze of floating aerial mats tints the sky high up. A giant flyer glides low over a swell. Composition: glassy
swell in the foreground, the flyer in the middle, the squall line and anvils on the close horizon.

No people, boats, ships, buildings, vehicles, spacecraft, text, labels, watermarks or borders. No red or orange sky. No
Earth animals.

## Variations

- Show a night-time storm over the open ocean before dawn, lit by lightning.
- Show the squall reaching the camera: whitecaps and rain hammering the swell.
