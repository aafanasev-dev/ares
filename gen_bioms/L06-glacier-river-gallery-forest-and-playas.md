# L06 · Glacier-river gallery forest and playas

Description: `biomes.md` → L06. Paste the text under **Prompt** into ChatGPT to generate the image.

## Prompt

Create a photorealistic 16:9 landscape photograph in the style of a nature documentary: drone shot with a wide-angle
lens, natural light, sharp detail, believable scale.

Setting: the planet Ares, real Mars terrain turned into an Earth-like world with oceans, NOT the red Mars of space
photos. The sky is blue with white clouds, the air is breathable, the sun looks the same size as from Earth and there
is no moon. Gravity is only 0.38 g, so life grows about 2.6× taller and lighter than on Earth: trees reach hundreds of
metres on huge buttresses, big animals walk slowly on long, very thin legs, and flyers have giant wingspans. The
planet is small, so the horizon is close. Rain falls in few but very heavy storms. All plants and animals are alien
but believable: no Earth species, no dinosaurs.

Scene: midday, following a river across a dry, cold high plateau toward the place where it dies into a salt flat. The
river is milky and braided, much wider and shallower than an Earth river of the same flow, and comes from a distant
ice-capped shield volcano on the horizon. It fades into a brilliant white salt playa holding a shrinking, shallow lake.
A dense green ribbon of gallery forest 20–60 m tall hugs the water and nothing else; reed beds and salt-tolerant scrub
fringe the playa. Outside the ribbon, the land is pale, dry steppe. Grazing animals with long thin legs gather at the
water's edge, a predator waits motionless in the reeds, and flyers nest in the treetops. Bright, dry air, heat shimmer
over the salt, one small cloud clinging to the distant ice cap. Composition: the green river ribbon leading the eye
from the foreground to the white playa and the volcano behind.

No people, buildings, roads, vehicles, spacecraft, text, labels, watermarks or borders. No red or orange sky. No Earth
animals.

## Variations

- Show dawn: valley fog lies along the river and frost covers the steppe.
- Show a failed melt year: the river low, trees leafless, the playa cracked and white.
