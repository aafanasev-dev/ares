## Prompt

Create a photorealistic 16:9 landscape photograph in the style of a nature documentary: drone shot hovering out in
mid-air beside a cliff, wide-angle lens, natural light, sharp detail, believable scale.

Setting: the planet Ares, real Mars terrain turned into an Earth-like world with oceans, NOT the red Mars of space
photos. The sky is blue with white clouds, the air is breathable, the sun looks the same size as from Earth and there
is no moon. Gravity is only 0.38 g, so life grows about 2.6× taller and lighter than on Earth: trees reach hundreds of
metres on huge buttresses, big animals walk slowly on long, very thin legs, and flyers have giant wingspans. The
planet is small, so the horizon is close. Rain falls in few but very heavy storms. All plants and animals are alien
but believable: no Earth species, no dinosaurs.

Scene: midday at a waterfall 1,200 m high pouring over the rim of an enormous canyon. In the weak gravity the falling
water breaks up long before it lands and turns into a slow, drifting white column of mist, so there is no plunge pool
and no impact at the bottom, only a drenched apron of pale boulders lost in fog. The mist drifts sideways for
kilometres and soaks the canyon walls, which are vertical rock hung with thick moss, cushion plants and heavy
epiphytes growing straight on the stone, dripping everywhere, with no soil. Rainbows hang in the spray. Above the rim
is dark cloud forest; below, the canyon floor disappears into haze. Flyers with giant wingspans ride the updraught
beside the column. Cool, saturated, deeply shadowed air with shafts of sun through the mist. Composition: the white
mist column falling through the frame from top to bottom, the wet green wall beside it giving the scale.

No people, buildings, roads, vehicles, spacecraft, text, labels, watermarks or borders. No red or orange sky. No Earth
animals.

## Variations

- Show a monsoon flood in a steep upper gorge instead: a brown wall of water tearing between cloud-forest slopes.
- Show low sun at dawn: the mist column lit gold against a dark canyon still in shadow.
