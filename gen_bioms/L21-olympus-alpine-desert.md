# L21 · Olympus alpine desert

Description: `biomes.md` → L21. Paste the text under **Prompt** into ChatGPT to generate the image.

## Prompt

Create a photorealistic 16:9 landscape photograph in the style of a nature documentary: wide-angle lens, natural
light, sharp detail, believable scale.

Setting: the planet Ares, real Mars terrain turned into an Earth-like world with oceans, NOT the red Mars of space
photos. The sky is blue with white clouds, the air is breathable, the sun looks the same size as from Earth and there
is no moon. Gravity is only 0.38 g, so life grows about 2.6× taller and lighter than on Earth: trees reach hundreds of
metres on huge buttresses, big animals walk slowly on long, very thin legs, and flyers have giant wingspans. The
planet is small, so the horizon is close. Rain falls in few but very heavy storms. All plants and animals are alien
but believable: no Earth species, no dinosaurs.

Scene: noon about 8 km up on the flank of the highest volcano on the planet, an island rising out of the ocean. The
slope is gentle but endless: bare black and dark-red lava flows, cinder fields and scree, with only sparse, low scrub
in sheltered hollows at the bottom of the frame and nothing at all higher up. Uphill, the white edge of the summit ice
cap shines against a sky that is deeper blue than at sea level. Downhill, far below, a flat layer of white cloud covers
the forest belt, with the ocean showing through gaps and a close, curved horizon. The air is very clear, thin and
cold; frost lingers in the shadows of lava blocks. A single flyer glides below the viewpoint. Composition: lava blocks
with frost in the foreground, the endless bare slope in the middle, cloud sea and ocean far below.

No people, buildings, roads, vehicles, spacecraft, text, labels, watermarks or borders. No red or orange sky. No Earth
animals.

## Variations

- Show frost at dawn: every lava block rimmed white, long shadows.
- Show a passing snow shower dusting the black lava.
