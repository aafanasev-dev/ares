# S04 · Tharsis tip-jet seas

## Prompt

Create a photorealistic 16:9 seascape photograph in the style of a nature documentary: wide-angle lens low over the
water, natural light, sharp detail, believable scale.

Setting: the planet Ares, real Mars terrain turned into an Earth-like world with oceans, NOT the red Mars of space
photos. The sky is blue with white clouds, the air is breathable, the sun looks the same size as from Earth and there
is no moon. Gravity is only 0.38 g: ocean waves grow 1.6–2.6× taller and 2.6× longer than on Earth for the same wind
and roll in slowly with very long periods, spray hangs in the air, tides are tiny, and seabird-like flyers have giant
wingspans. The planet is small, so the horizon is close. All life is alien but believable: no Earth species.

Scene: a bright, cool day offshore at the tip of an enormous plateau continent, where the trade winds split and
accelerate into a permanent jet. In the distance, the dark edge of the plateau rises from the sea. Across the water
runs a sharply bounded strip of strong wind: inside it the sea is short, steep and chaotic, with waves 5–15 m high,
breaking crests and long streaks of blown spray; outside it the water is calmer. The churned water is streaked with
green bands of plankton brought up from below, and dozens of giant flyers wheel and dive to feed along the strip.
Composition: steep breaking seas and spray in the foreground, feeding flyers over green streaks in the middle, the
plateau edge on the horizon.

No people, boats, ships, buildings, vehicles, spacecraft, text, labels, watermarks or borders. No red or orange sky. No
Earth animals.

## Variations

- Show the jet strip from high above as a white, wind-streaked band cutting across a calm blue sea.
- Show evening light with spray glowing gold against dark water.
