## Prompt

Create a photorealistic 16:9 landscape photograph in the style of a nature documentary: high aerial shot with a
wide-angle lens, natural light, sharp detail, believable scale.

Setting: the planet Ares, real Mars terrain turned into an Earth-like world with oceans, NOT the red Mars of space
photos. The sky is blue with white clouds, the air is breathable, the sun looks the same size as from Earth and there
is no moon. Gravity is only 0.38 g, so life grows about 2.6× taller and lighter than on Earth: trees reach hundreds of

planet is small, so the horizon is close. Rain falls in few but very heavy storms. All plants and animals are alien
but believable: no Earth species, no dinosaurs.

Scene: noon, looking across the summit of a gigantic shield volcano about 16 km high. A smooth ice cap roughly 5 km
thick fills and overflows a huge summit caldera ringed by dark lava cliffs. Slow, sluggish glaciers creep down the
mountain's gentle flanks, ending far below in meltwater streams where a faint green-brown band of life begins. At the
lower, melting edge of the ice there are faint pinkish-green microbial stains; the ice itself is lifeless. At this
height the sky is a deep, dark blue, the ice is blinding white with sharp shadows, and a thin plume of blowing snow
trails off the rim. Beyond the mountain, a white cloud deck covers the land and the close horizon is visibly curved.
A single giant flyer glides far below the summit, a speck for scale. Composition: caldera rim cliffs in the
foreground, the vast ice cap in the middle, glacier tongues and cloud deck beyond.

No people, buildings, roads, vehicles, spacecraft, text, labels, watermarks or borders. No red or orange sky. No Earth
animals.
