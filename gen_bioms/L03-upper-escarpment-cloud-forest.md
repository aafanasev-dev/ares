## Prompt

Create a photorealistic 16:9 landscape photograph in the style of a nature documentary: wide-angle lens, soft natural
light, sharp detail, believable scale.

Setting: the planet Ares, real Mars terrain turned into an Earth-like world with oceans, NOT the red Mars of space
photos. The sky is blue with white clouds, the air is breathable, the sun looks the same size as from Earth and there
is no moon. Gravity is only 0.38 g, so life grows about 2.6× taller and lighter than on Earth: trees reach hundreds of
metres on huge buttresses, big animals walk slowly on long, very thin legs, and flyers have giant wingspans. The
planet is small, so the horizon is close. Rain falls in few but very heavy storms. All plants and animals are alien
but believable: no Earth species, no dinosaurs.

Scene: dawn, 4–5 km up near the top of a colossal mountain wall, at the moment a brief gap opens in the cloud. In the
foreground, a dwarf moss forest: gnarled, low trees thick with dripping moss and epiphytes, water falling from every
branch. Below, steep ridges and gorges fall away into a sea of cloud, with 60–100 m trees visible in the gullies.
Uphill, the forest thins to wind-pruned scrub and then stops abruptly at a sharp rim, beyond which lies dry, pale
grassland of a high plateau under clear sky — a rain shadow starting exactly at the crest. A giant soaring flyer with
a wingspan around 20 m rides the updraft along the wall with motionless wings. Cool, dripping fog, diffuse pearly
light, first sunlight touching the rim. Composition: wet moss forest in the foreground, cloud sea below, dry rim in the
background.

No people, buildings, roads, vehicles, spacecraft, text, labels, watermarks or borders. No red or orange sky. No Earth
animals.

## Variations

- Show full cloud immersion: only nearby moss-draped trunks are visible, glowing in fog.
- Show the monsoon season: cloud base far below, rain streaming down the wall.
