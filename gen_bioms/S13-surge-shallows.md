## Prompt

Create a photorealistic 16:9 seascape photograph in the style of a nature documentary: drone shot with a wide-angle
lens, natural light, sharp detail, believable scale.

Setting: the planet Ares, real Mars terrain turned into an Earth-like world with oceans, NOT the red Mars of space
photos. The sky is blue with white clouds, the air is breathable, the sun looks the same size as from Earth and there
is no moon. Gravity is only 0.38 g: ocean waves grow 1.6–2.6× taller and 2.6× longer than on Earth for the same wind
and roll in slowly with very long periods, spray hangs in the air, tides are tiny, and seabird-like flyers have giant
wingspans. The planet is small, so the horizon is close. All life is alien but believable: no Earth species.

Scene: a warm, sunny day over a shallow tropical sea with an archipelago of very flat, low islands barely above the
water. The sea changes from deep blue to bright turquoise over shallow banks, where long trade-wind waves 3–5 m high
break in white lines. The islands are pale, damp salt flats with glistening crust and pink-green brine lagoons —
almost nothing grows on them. A thin sheet of seawater has spilled far inland over one island after a recent surge.
Humid haze softens the distance. Flyers wade in a lagoon. Composition: breaking waves on a bank in the foreground, a
salt island with coloured lagoons in the middle, more low islands fading into haze.

No people, boats, ships, buildings, vehicles, spacecraft, text, labels, watermarks or borders. No red or orange sky. No
Earth animals.

## Variations

- Show a storm surge: a 25 m wall of water sweeping over the flat islands.
- Show evening: calm lagoons reflecting a pastel sky over the salt.
