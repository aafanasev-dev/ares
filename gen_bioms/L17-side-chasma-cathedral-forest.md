## Prompt

Create a photorealistic 16:9 landscape photograph in the style of a nature documentary: ultra-wide lens from the forest
floor looking up and along the canyon, soft natural light, sharp detail, believable scale.

Setting: the planet Ares, real Mars terrain turned into an Earth-like world with oceans, NOT the red Mars of space
photos. The sky is blue with white clouds, the air is breathable, the sun looks the same size as from Earth and there
is no moon. Gravity is only 0.38 g, so life grows about 2.6× taller and lighter than on Earth: trees reach hundreds of
metres on huge buttresses, big animals walk slowly on long, very thin legs, and flyers have giant wingspans. The
planet is small, so the horizon is close. Rain falls in few but very heavy storms. All plants and animals are alien
but believable: no Earth species, no dinosaurs.

Scene: midday in a sheltered side canyon of a gigantic flooded canyon system, where the air is dead calm, warm and
permanently misted. Canyon walls rise 4 km on both sides and vanish into the mist. On the canyon floor, beside still
dark water, stands the tallest forest imaginable: trunks 12 m thick rise 300 m into a canopy lost in haze, with
enormous buttresses, hanging mosses, vines and heavy epiphytes; the forest floor is very dark. A stilt-wader 60–80 m
tall on four absurdly thin legs browses at mid-canopy height, and gliding animals drift between the trunks. High above
the canopy, huge catenary sky-nets hundreds of metres across are strung between the canyon walls, glittering with
droplets and holding trapped drifting green mats. Soft shafts of light pierce the mist. Composition: giant buttress
roots in the foreground, stilt-wader among trunks in the middle, sky-nets and walls fading into mist above.

No people, buildings, roads, vehicles, spacecraft, text, labels, watermarks or borders. No red or orange sky. No Earth
animals.
