# S02 · Northern trade-wind ocean

Description: `biomes.md` → S02. Paste the text under **Prompt** into ChatGPT to generate the image.

## Prompt

Create a photorealistic 16:9 seascape photograph in the style of a nature documentary: wide-angle lens a few metres
above the waves, natural light, sharp detail, believable scale.

Setting: the planet Ares, real Mars terrain turned into an Earth-like world with oceans, NOT the red Mars of space
photos. The sky is blue with white clouds, the air is breathable, the sun looks the same size as from Earth and there
is no moon. Gravity is only 0.38 g: ocean waves grow 1.6–2.6× taller and 2.6× longer than on Earth for the same wind
and roll in slowly with very long periods, spray hangs in the air, tides are tiny, and seabird-like flyers have giant
wingspans. The planet is small, so the horizon is close. All life is alien but believable: no Earth species.

Scene: a sunny day in the open ocean under steady easterly trade winds that have blown across 12,000 km of water. The
sea is covered in the largest regular waves on the planet: long, marching ridges 4–5.5 m high with a 19-second period,
each crest far from the next, tipped with whitecaps and trailing slow spray. Neat rows of small trade-wind cumulus
drift overhead. The water is a clear, deep ocean blue. A giant seabird-like flyer skims the face of a wave with
motionless wings. Composition: a long wave face in the foreground, the flyer in the middle, rows of swells and cumulus
to the close horizon.

No people, boats, ships, buildings, vehicles, spacecraft, text, labels, watermarks or borders. No red or orange sky. No
Earth animals.

## Variations

- Show sunset: backlit wave crests glowing gold.
- Show a view from high above: parallel swell lines covering the ocean to the horizon.
